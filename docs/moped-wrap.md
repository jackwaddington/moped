# Moped Colour-Flip Wrap

Notes for wrapping the moped (**SYM Mask 50**, 50cc) in a colour-shift / flip vinyl.

## Feasibility summary

- **Been done before?** Yes — wrapping 50cc scooters/mopeds is common, both DIY and
  by pro shops. Small bike = small job vs a car. Plenty of YouTube walk-throughs for
  scooter side panels / fairings.
- **Reversible?** Yes. Cast vinyl on factory paint in good condition peels off cleanly
  with gentle heat (heat gun / hairdryer), no residue. Risk to paint is low unless the
  paint is already flaking, aftermarket/resprayed, or the wrap is left on past its life
  (~5-7 yrs for the Color Shift cast films; the Neon Chrome films are only ~1 yr).
- **Difficulty:** SYM Mask has fairly flat, boxy panels (leg shield, side covers, front
  fairing, rear panel, front mudguard) — a forgiving first wrap. Removable panels can
  be unbolted and wrapped on a bench. Air-channel adhesive on the Teckwrap films lets
  you reposition and squeegee out bubbles.
- **Material type matters:** stick to the **Color Shift RD-series / DS-series cast PVC**
  (0.13-0.15mm) for the curved bits. Avoid the **Neon Chrome MCH** films on anything
  curved — 0.27mm, tears/lifts on compound curves, short lifespan.

## Kit needed

- Heat gun (or good hairdryer), felt-edged squeegee, sharp snap-off knife, tape measure,
  isopropyl alcohol + microfibre for prep, gloves. Optional: cutting tape for clean edges.

## Rough cost (Color Shift film @ €70/m of 152cm)

- A whole 50cc scooter typically takes ~1.5m × 5m (≈ 5ft × 16ft) of film.
- On qzvinyls that's ~4-5 running metres of the 152cm film ≈ **€280-350** plus tools.
- A partial wrap (side panels + front fairing only) could be done in ~2-2.5m ≈ €140-175.
